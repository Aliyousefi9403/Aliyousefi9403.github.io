   I'm a Computer Science student at City St George's, University of London, and I'm starting a public log of my cybersecurity learning.

   ## Why publicly?

   Two reasons. First, writing about something forces me to actually understand it — not just watch a video and move on. Second, I want a real record I can point to when I apply for MSc programmes and internships later this year.

   ## Where I'm starting

   I completed the TryHackMe *Offensive Security Intro* room today and I'm halfway through the *Complete Beginner* path. Next up:

   - Finish the TryHackMe *Complete Beginner* and *Cyber Security 101* paths
   - Start studying for CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)
   - Work through Harvard CS50 Cybersecurity
   - Read papers on LLMs used for cyber attacks (a reading list from Dr Nikos Komninos)

   ## The plan

   I'm aiming to build a strong enough foundation over the next 8 weeks to have a real dissertation project in place by term start, sit the Security+ exam, and start seriously applying for MSc programmes and internships in cybersecurity.

   More posts as I go.
